### Relay Impedance & Amplifier Operating Voltage
* **Relay Burden Analysis:** Based on the SEL relay's specifications (`$0.27\text{ VA}$` at `$5\text{ A}$` nominal $\approx 0.0108\text{ }\Omega$, and `$2.51\text{ VA}$` at `$15\text{ A}$` $\approx 0.011\text{ }\Omega$), the relay's impedance varies by only `$\approx 0.3\text{ m}\Omega$` across the operating range.
* **Load Resistor Reduction:** Because the relay impedance is nearly constant, the team can reduce the series load resistor from `$1\text{ }\Omega$` down to `$0.01\text{ }\Omega$`–`$0.05\text{ }\Omega$`, significantly lowering power dissipation on the load resistor (down to `$\approx 1.62\text{ W}$`).
* **Voltage Confirmation (LTspice):** The team briefly considered dropping the power supply rails to `$\pm 5\text{ V}$`. However, LTspice simulations showed that `$\pm 5\text{ V}$` caused the output sine wave to clip into a square wave due to op-amp operating limits.
* **Final Power Requirement:** Confirmed via LTspice and the component datasheet that the amplifiers still require `$\pm 15\text{ V}$` power supplies (with a `$0.05\text{ }\Omega$` load resistance) to produce a clean `$18\text{ A}$` peak sine wave. The team still requires non-internally-grounded or split-rail `$\pm 15\text{ V}$` power supplies.

### SEL Relay Settings & Discrepancy
* **Settings Received:** Reviewed Daniel's QuickSet settings (`1200:5` CT ratio, PB9/PB10 for close/trip, hot line tag, SCADA remote control logic, and two settings groups).
* **Current Limit Mismatch:** Daniel's QuickSet file has the phase instantaneous overcurrent (`50P1P`) pickup set to `15`. The team confirmed via the manual that QuickSet uses **RMS** values, meaning `$15\text{ A}_{\text{RMS}}$` equates to over `$21\text{ A}$` peak—exceeding the previously agreed-upon `$18\text{ A}$` peak (`$\approx 12.7\text{ A}_{\text{RMS}}$`).
