## 2. Research and Existing Solutions

- Research indicates that no single readily available commercial unit exactly combines all desired functions. Existing products tend to specialize in relay testing, circuit-breaker simulation, coordination testing, or related functions.
- The team discussed combining these capabilities into a student-friendly, plug-and-play, repeatable training bench incorporating relay settings, simulated operating conditions/faults, breaker feedback, and recorded-event data.
- A major technical challenge is determining how to inject the required voltage/current into the SEL relay so realistic test cases can be performed and recorded.
- The research direction was divided into two major areas: the injection method and the testing/recording system.
- The Omicron CMC 500 was identified as a commercial multi-phase protection-relay test set. A quote request was submitted to Omicron.
- A low-voltage breaker simulator from Barrington/CBS-series equipment was also identified as a possible way to provide simulated breaker behavior.

## 3. Technical Scope and Design Questions

- The working concept is to provide an appropriate current source to the relay and a mechanism that simulates breaker operation/feedback.
- Two possible approaches were discussed: reduce the required current to an economically practical level, or use transformer/power-conversion equipment if higher current is required.
- The team wants to determine whether the SEL relay can operate with sufficiently low current/voltage inputs to avoid purchasing an expensive high-current source.
- The team discussed scaling industry-standard current values for a training application so the maximum current on an individual phase remains within practical laboratory limits.
- The team also discussed whether breaker-simulator voltage requirements can be supplied directly from relay outputs or whether rectification, transformation, or other circuitry would be required.
- The SEL relay specification sheet should be reviewed to determine its applicable input ranges and ratings. This will directly affect the current-source design and budget.
- Hot-line-tag functionality is expected to be part of the project. The team believes the SEL relay has a built-in hot-line-tag function, but its exact operation and any required configuration/modification need to be verified.
