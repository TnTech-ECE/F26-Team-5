## Detailed Design & Technical Decisions
* **Visual Representation:** The project requires a visual buildable model, prompting a debate between designing a custom PCB or using a manual solderable project board.
* **PCB vs. Protoboard:** A custom PCB offers a cleaner layout but carries a high risk of project failure if the board malfunctions. A manual solderable board is more labor-intensive but allows for dynamic component replacement and troubleshooting.
* **Thermal Management:** The nine amplifier chips will run at approximately 5 amps and 15 volts with 80% efficiency, dissipating roughly 45 Watts collectively. The team is investigating whether the project's 19-inch metal enclosure box can serve as the primary heatsink.
* **Thick Film Resistors:** The selected 100W thick film load resistor will require dedicated heatsinking.
* **Power Supply:** The low-voltage DC power supply will connect to both the microcontroller and the amplifiers.
* **Wireless Connections:** The team formally dropped the idea of connecting to the microcontroller wirelessly due to high failure risks and unnecessary complexity.
