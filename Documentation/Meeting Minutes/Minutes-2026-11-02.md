## 2. Key Discussions and Decisions

### Document Formatting and Version Control
* **Issue:** The team experienced significant formatting issues using Microsoft Word's equation editor (jumping text, strange formatting blocks, incorrect auto-corrects for "current injection").
* **Decision:** The team will minimize the use of Word's equation tool where it causes instability, fix the formatting manually, and ensure everything is properly converted to Markdown for the final GitHub repository.

### Introduction & Scope Changes (Travis & Alexander)
* **Issue:** The project scope has changed, and the team discussed where to introduce this context.
* **Decision:** Travis will add a brief statement at the very beginning of the Introduction to acknowledge the scope change. This will seamlessly lead into Alexander's "Restating the Fully Formulated Problem" section without needing to cut or heavily edit existing text.

### Specifications and "Shall" Statements
* **Issue:** Strict wording in "shall" statements could cause the team to fail criteria if exact values aren't perfectly met (e.g., exactly 120-degree phase angles or exactly 18.0 amps).
* **Decision:** Added the word "approximately" to constraints (e.g., "approximately 18 amps," "offset by approximately $120^{\circ}$") to allow for realistic hardware tolerances while still meeting customer (Daniel/Storm) expectations.
* **Additional Note:** The OMICRON CMC will be explicitly mentioned as a potential solution but dismissed due to its high cost to satisfy the requirement of comparing potential solutions.

### High-Level Solution
* **Clarification:** The document needs to clearly state that the AC-to-DC conversion powers the STM microcontroller, the DAC, and the amplifiers. The SEL-351S relay itself will be powered separately (wall power).

### Amplification Subsystem and LT Spice Simulations (Alexander & Hayden)
* **Issue:** LT Spice simulations using a $1\Omega$ load resistor showed the op-amps clipping at 10A and experiencing current sharing imbalances. 
* **Discussion:** To reach the 18A peak, the input voltage from the microcontroller needs to be carefully scaled (e.g., 0.36V peak input). The internal resistance of the SEL relay is unknown but expected to be very low. 
* **Decision:** The team will keep the $1\Omega$ resistor in the conceptual design for now, as it theoretically works and simplifies calculations. However, the team acknowledges this may need to be swapped for a $0.5\Omega$ or lower resistor during the Detailed Design phase to avoid clipping and thermal issues. 

### Standards and Ethical Considerations
* **Decision:** The team agreed to keep all listed standards (NFPA, NEC, IEEE, IEC) in the document. Even if some seem excessive, it is better to be thorough and "cover all bases" regarding safety and harmonic control.

### Budget constraints (John)
* **Status:** The budget is extremely tight. The low-end estimate currently leaves only \$0.85 remaining of the \$1,000 budget.
* **Decision:** The team confirmed the \$195 cost for the two 15V, 500W power supplies. To keep costs down, the team will attempt to source standard materials (like 10 AWG wire) from existing lab supplies or scrap rather than purchasing them new.
